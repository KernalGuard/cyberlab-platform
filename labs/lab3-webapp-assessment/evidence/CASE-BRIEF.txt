Variant: demo

Client:   Cedarwick Neighborhood Aid (fictional nonprofit)
Context:  After the incident you investigated in Labs 1 and 2, Cedarwick's board wants its
          donation website (give.cedarwick.test) assessed before it relaunches.

AUTHORISATION (scope letter, signed by the Executive Director)
  In scope:   the lab copy of the site only: http://127.0.0.1:8080 from your computer,
              http://gateway:8080 from the lab workstation.
  Out of scope: everything else. No other hosts, sites or networks.
  Rules:      No denial of service. Password guessing ONLY with wordlist.txt, one attempt
              per word per account. Do not modify or delete other users' data. Quote no
              more than three donor records in your report.
  Goal:       Identify and validate weaknesses, rate their risk, recommend fixes, and fix
              one (the remediation exercise) with proof that it works.

Evidence: seed.sql      the site's starting data (loaded automatically; do NOT read it to
                        find answers: findings must come from testing the site)
          wordlist.txt  the only passwords you may try against staff logins
          MANIFEST.sha256

This site and its data are SYNTHETIC and intentionally vulnerable. Run it only on your
own computer, bound to 127.0.0.1. Do not use these techniques on any other system.
